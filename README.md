# Nestling: newborn photo studio website (concept)

Concept website and booking flow for Nestling, an imagined newborn photo studio in London.

Parents book by their due date. The studio holds the ten days when babies are 5 to 14 days old, and the exact day is fixed once the baby arrives.

**Live prototype:** https://irynachelombitko.github.io/nestling/ On a phone the home page and booking steps switch to the mobile layout.

## Pages
- Home: hero, why the first two weeks, recent sessions, packages, booking by due date, the studio, before you come
- Gallery
- The studio
- Booking: package, dates, your details, done
- Mobile: home, package, dates

## About
Design: Iryna Chelombitko, UX/UI designer. [Behance](https://www.behance.net/Iryna_chelombitko)

This is a portfolio concept, not a real business. The phone number, address and bookings are made up and the photos are AI-generated.
