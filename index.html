Before you come

Nothing to buy and nothing to rehearse. Four small things make the day easier.

Read the full guide
  • Feed your baby just before you leave home.
  • Dress them in something that opens at the front.
  • Wear light layers. We keep the rooms warm.
  • Bring a dummy if you use one. We go at your baby's pace.
