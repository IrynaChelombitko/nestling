The night before

We text you the evening before with the time and the door code. If it was a hard night, reply and we can start an hour later.

Getting here

Unit 3, Marlow Yard, London E8, six minutes on foot from Hackney Central. The door is step-free and the pram comes inside with you. There is one parking bay outside: ask for it when you book.

Feed and dress

  • Feed your baby just before you leave home.
  • Dress them in something that opens at the front.
  • Wear light layers. We keep the rooms at 26°C.
  • Bring a dummy if you use one. We go at your baby's pace.

During the session

Sessions run at your baby's pace. Feeds, changes and cuddles are part of the time, and we stop the clock for a long feed. We only use poses that are safe for a newborn: when a photo looks like your baby is holding their own head, it is two photos joined together, with your hand supporting them in each.

After the session

Within two weeks you get a private online gallery. You pick your photos there: 10, 20 or 30, depending on your package. Extra photos are £12 each.

If plans change

If your baby comes early or late, your dates move for free. The £50 deposit is refundable until 14 days before your due date.

Questions before you book? Text us on 020 7946 0321.
